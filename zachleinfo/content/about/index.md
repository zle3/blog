## Introduction

I am based in Sacramento, CA, and currently work as a Network and Systems Support Engineer on contract with CSI Consulting Group. Before that, I spent more than three years in California state government IT, most recently as a Senior Desktop Support Engineer at the Department of Consumer Affairs, where a significant part of my work involved automating provisioning and deployment with PowerShell across Active Directory, Microsoft Entra ID, and SCCM.

Outside of work, I run a multi-host Proxmox home lab and host the production environment for an AI-built gaming community platform, which I write about in the [blog](/blog/) and [portfolio](/portfolio/). I am finishing a B.S. in Cloud and Network Engineering at Western Governors University (expected February 2027). My full work history is on the [resume](/resume/) page.

## Recent Achievements

**At work**

- Started a new role in October 2026 managing network and file share access, Exchange Online groups, and 40 shared mailboxes for 150 users, and standardized tracking and procurement for 200 devices.
- Cut account provisioning time by 20 minutes and removed about an hour of manual application and patch installation per endpoint with PowerShell at the Department of Consumer Affairs.
- Reduced support volume by 89 tickets over a two-month tracking period by driving root cause analysis on repeat issues, and migrated domain controllers to Windows Server 2022 across two production sites.
- Recovered $10,000 in annual licensing costs with PowerShell automation for Active Directory user lifecycle and Microsoft 365 access.
- Reduced Windows Autopilot deployment from 20 technician steps to 4, an 80% cut in endpoint deployment time, and stood up a ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus platform handling roughly 800 tickets a month.

**Outside of work**

- **AI-driven gaming community platform:** founded and run a self-hosted platform for an Aion 2 community, with a Rust desktop app, a members website, and a Discord bot. A team of 29 Claude Code agents built it under change-control rules I designed, merging 945 pull requests in 29 days, and it reached 54 monthly active app installs within about a month of launch. The full story is in the [blog series](/blog/2026/ai-agent-platform-part-1) and on the [portfolio page](/portfolio/2026/ai-community-platform).
- **Home lab automation:** replaced 9 manual bash scripts with a 6-role Ansible playbook covering firewall rules, cron, Docker Compose applications, Cloudflare Tunnel access, and NAS backups, written up in the [Palworld series](/blog/2026/self-hosted-palworld-part-4).
- **Cloud delivery pipeline:** provisioned a containerized app on Google Cloud Run with Terraform and a Cloud Build CI/CD pipeline ([demo](/portfolio/2026/gcp-terraform-demo)).
